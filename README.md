One short family scene in two lines: a father still sets the table for four, years after his daughter moved away — and the Sunday she comes home, sits in her old chair, and tells him the food tastes better here, he cries into the soup and blames the onions.

> Dad still sets the table for four, though his daughter moved away years ago.
> Last Sunday she came home, sat in her old chair, and said the food tasted better here — and he cried into the soup and blamed the onions.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the scene to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 280 characters of UTF-8 text, one or two lines.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
